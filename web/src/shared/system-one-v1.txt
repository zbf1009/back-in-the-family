How to read the facts:
- Relations are directed. A line "#1 -> #2 affinity A trust T tags parent" means #2 is #1's parent, not that #1 is #2's parent. Tags say what the second person (#2) is to the first person (#1). The first person is the one who feels it.
- Magnitude units: dollars for money events (loan, repayment, deal and similar), joss paper sheet counts for offerings, and intensity from 1 to 100 for social events. 0 means not applicable.
- For underworld officials (origin official), alive means in office, not living.
- One tick is one day. Every "day N" in the facts counts ticks, and a "due day N" is the day a loan or promise falls due. Compare it with the current day given in the user text.
- Event actors come in a fixed order. loan: lender, then borrower. repayment: payer, then payee. promise and promise_kept: promisor, then promisee. deal: seller, then buyer. debt_transfer: previous debtor, then new debtor. debt_forgiven: creditor, then debtor. will: testator, then beneficiary. inheritance: deceased, then heir. insult, help, gift and theft: doer, then target. accident: the one responsible, then the one hurt. apology: the one apologizing, then the one addressed. marriage and courtship: the two people. birth, death and illness: the person.

How to choose:
- Weigh the person's traits, their relations to the others involved, and the debts, promises and events in the log. Prefer the option that fits what the facts show about this person and these relations. Do not pick an option that ignores a debt, promise or bond the facts show, unless the facts give a reason.
