## Glossary

- **Author**: the project owner who approved local delivery/trial and the named combined release/finite-model-use grant, and separately confirms any prompt revision.
- **Obituary**: a presentation-only card for the player's own death, titled exactly `Obituary`, retained in that life's history.
- **Closed_Facts**: the frozen, evidence-backed obituary facts defined in `design.md`: name, native age at death, formatted death date, human origin, required complete-known surviving spouse/children/grandchildren and predeceased spouses/parents/children/siblings from the native death-time summary, same-life debt aggregates, recorded distributed estate valuation/actual beneficiaries, and distinct same-day offerers.
- **Author_Template**: the exact fixed text, omissions, singular/plural variants and relation/list formatting in `target/life-unfolds/copy-deck.md` section 14; this Spec does not authorize editorial substitutes.
- **Narration_Attempt**: the presentation ledger's one-shot operation for an actual own death, using the judge narration contract in `design.md`, not a gameplay decision.
- **Obituary_Sidecar**: a separate version-1 `.obituaries.json` file bound to the SHA-256 of the exact original replay UTF-8 bytes; it is not a replay-v5 field.
- **Technical_Details**: the existing technical surface for provenance, fallback and export diagnostics; these are not labels on the Obituary.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Factual, readable own-death card — OB-R1

**User Story:** As the Author, I want to finish or skip my character's death performance and read a clear Obituary about that life, without losing access to the game.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the player's own death performance settles, including Skip or reduced-motion settlement, and its terminal obituary text is ready, THE Web_App SHALL show one Obituary for that actual death, without displaying custom loading copy or provisional text that is later overwritten.
2. WHEN building an Obituary, THE Web_App SHALL freeze Closed_Facts from the complete admitted death-day batch, that body's life records and its native pre-edge-destruction own-death kin summary, carrying every required complete-known family array and real name/sex; `[]` SHALL mean known empty only, while missing-summary delivery SHALL be repaired rather than accepted as missing family. Native kin SHALL be unique by PersonID per category; the ID-free model kin and beneficiary arrays SHALL retain distinct people's same-name/same-relation entries in stable native order without merging, renaming or exposing IDs.
3. WHEN using Author_Template, THE Web_App SHALL reproduce section 14 exactly, including all three origin-specific opening lines without whole-line omission, omission of the whole offering line for zero, singular/plural variants, bare relation-plus-first-name lists, known-sex or neutral relation wording, and the unconditional final line, without parentheses, `you` or history qualifiers.
4. WHILE an obituary attempt is pending or a card is displayed, THE Web_App SHALL preserve the existing death scene's settlement and all legal game controls, without a narration tick hold, automatic scene selection or automatic advancement.
5. THE Obituary SHALL keep its exact title and readable text accessible by keyboard and at narrow width and 200% zoom, with no visible source/model/fallback labels; diagnostic information SHALL remain in Technical_Details.

### Requirement 2: One validated judge narration, exact fallback — OB-R2

**User Story:** As the Author, I want the judge site to show one restrained, factual paragraph when narration succeeds and the same fixed Obituary template when it cannot, while public play stays model-free.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an authenticated judge death is eligible under the current capability/inference policy, THE Web_App SHALL start its one-shot Narration_Attempt immediately after the complete observed death-day batch is admitted, using only the design-defined closed-facts JSON and exact Author system prompt through the existing AI binding, without waiting for the death performance or holding ticks.
2. IF play is public, unauthenticated, disabled or narration is unavailable, THEN THE Web_App SHALL use Author_Template; public play SHALL issue neither the narration POST nor a model request.
3. WHEN a model result arrives, THE server and Web_App SHALL accept only the design-defined response string satisfying the plain single-paragraph, 40–110-word, at-most-1,200-Unicode-scalar and no-HTML/Markdown/control-character guards, forbidden-claim checks, and the same name rule for every uppercase-initial word anywhere, including sentence starts: a normalized input name token/alias or the design's fixed comprehensive ordinary-English starter/content vocabulary, including Year, Day, Halloween, Underworld, Bureau and Magistrate. Fixed vocabulary lookup SHALL be case-insensitive and possessive `'s` SHALL be stripped only for comparison; no arbitrary first-word exemption or short 16-word whitelist SHALL apply. Accepted words SHALL be displayed unchanged; uncertain or invalid output SHALL be rejected rather than repaired or sent to another model, without claiming full semantic proof.
4. IF authentication/configuration/body/admission/provider/output validation fails or either terminal deadline is reached, THEN THE Narration_Attempt SHALL freeze Author_Template as its terminal text and SHALL neither retry nor replace that text with a later provider result.
5. THE narration route SHALL use the shared boundaries' atomic duplicate claim and unchanged judge admission pool, preserving the existing decision path and counters while ensuring that duplicate own-death requests cannot create another admission or AI invocation.
6. WHEN an accepted result arrives within the deadline after its death performance has settled, THE Web_App SHALL reveal it then; IF that life is no longer current, THEN THE Web_App SHALL retain it with that frozen historical death without commandeering the new life's current scene.

### Requirement 3: In-memory terminal words and exact export — OB-R3

**User Story:** As the Author, I want the current page to retain each death's exact terminal words outside the simulation and export them alongside the unchanged replay, without any additional model request.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Web_App SHALL synchronously mark the attempt pending in a page-local in-memory per-run presentation ledger before dispatch, freeze terminal text, facts and provenance outside World/checksum before reveal, and accept only the first terminal transition. Duplicate facts/rerenders SHALL NOT dispatch again; refresh SHALL discard the run/ledger without browser persistence or resumption. Successful new World creation SHALL use a fresh memory run key, failed creation SHALL preserve the previous run, rebirth SHALL retain it, and old owned replies SHALL NOT mutate the current World.
2. WHEN exporting a replay, THE Web_App SHALL leave its existing bytes unchanged and SHALL offer a separate Obituary_Sidecar containing exact terminal text and metadata, bound to the SHA-256 of those exact original UTF-8 replay bytes, only when every own death within that export prefix is terminal. The ordinary replay export SHALL remain usable while narration is pending or sidecar export fails; neither export SHALL invoke a model or include session cookies. The sidecar SHALL preserve words for external or future consumers without adding a browser import or viewer in this Spec.
