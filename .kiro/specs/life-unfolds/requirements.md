## Glossary

- **Author**: the project owner who approves these drafts, evaluates the playable experience, and separately authorizes publication.
- **World**: the authoritative simulation state; one tick is one simulated day.
- **Fact record**: a read-only projection of an actual EventLog event or native Player Chronicle outcome, retaining identity, date, actors, and recorded causal links.
- **Life journal**: dated full-sentence presentation of fact records, separate from their storage and selection.
- **Close20**: kinship in either direction or absolute affinity of at least 20 in either direction, evaluated across the observed day's start/end focus; this is relevance, not a claim of friendship.
- **Current identity**: the player's actual body, or latest waiting ghost, with actual past-life rememberers; a soul's continuity does not imply recognition of a new body.
- **Key scene**: a seconds-long performance for a player decision, own death, rebirth, or own Dream; ordinary town events are journal entries, not individual cutscenes.
- **Technical area**: a collapsed disclosure for raw identifiers, schema, seed/configuration, replay, and audit information.
- **Admission / application**: an input being accepted into the existing queue / its actual later processing by the core; admission alone is not a completed effect.

## Requirement 1 — Understandable Player Language (LU-R1)

### User Story

As a player, I want to start a life and understand its dates, people, choices, pace, and results without reading simulation or audit terminology.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the player presses **Start**, THE interface SHALL generate an external random u64 seed on the main thread and call the existing create-world route; an advanced section SHALL offer an optional custom seed, blank-as-random behavior, and **Town size** from 40–60, default 50, rather than a primary raw World_Config JSON form. The actual seed SHALL be retained for replay/technical inspection; restarting a game SHALL be distinct from rebirth.
2. THE normal interface SHALL use one shared exhaustive copy module, with the Author deck's verbatim wording for all 24 player options, five node families, seven traits, six Dream intents, eight Dream outcomes, kinship, origin/rebirth paths, units, Needed conditions, rulings, sources, skipped parts and rejection labels. Names SHALL come from actual records: unknown journal names read **someone**, unknown document names read **Name unavailable**. Raw option/node/person/soul/body/tick/request/input identifiers, enumeration tags and audit jargon SHALL be absent from normal text, titles and accessible names; missing wording is reported to the Author, not replaced with a raw key.
3. THE interface SHALL display fictional 365-day years as Year = floor(tick/365) + 1 and Day = tick mod 365 + 1, with Day 1 labeled **Halloween** for the default calendar. Header dates SHALL use the reported current World day, entries SHALL retain their actual event day, and age SHALL use actual whole-year World age or known historical biological facts, without invented birthdays or arrival ages. u64/i64 dates and amounts SHALL be formatted from decimal strings/BigInt without Number rounding.
4. THE existing single advancement loop SHALL offer **Read — 1 day/second**, default **Life — 5 days/second**, pause, and one-day step, while preserving existing speeds at 30, 210, 900, 2,700, and 10,950 days/second with understandable labels. The 600–900-day IntroDebt rule SHALL remain unchanged; slower modes explicitly replace the former approximately 30-second first-stop presentation target.
5. WHEN a choice or action is shown, THE interface SHALL explain its meaning, available options, costs/conditions, and real received/processing/processed/refused state without changing internal values, keys, or callback arguments. IF no node is pending, THE interface SHALL NOT announce **A choice to make**; replay, raw configuration, identifiers, and audit detail SHALL remain available only in the collapsed technical area.
6. BEFORE Start, THE landing page SHALL use the exact premise and controls from copy-deck section 7, superseding the former three-sentence premise. AFTER successful Create, THE opening card SHALL use that section's exact lines and `story_opening`/current World facts for actual name, age, origin, living household and recorded family roles. Unsupported complete lines SHALL be omitted and reported, not rewritten or qualified; no missing relatives or facts are invented. The existing paused start remains, and the card SHALL NOT advance a day or add a pause reason.
7. WHEN the player invokes **Skip ahead** while paused or already playing, THE same advancement loop SHALL temporarily use **Years — 900 days/second** until the next existing stop, then restore the saved speed and leave that stop intact. A running world SHALL NOT receive another Run merely to accelerate; a paused world starts only through the existing guarded speed-ACK/Run route. It SHALL NOT answer a choice, skip a stop, add a pause reason or change rules. Pause/error/cancellation SHALL restore the same-world saved speed; explicit speed selection SHALL keep the newly selected speed, and successful new World creation SHALL discard old temporary state. Existing pending Run/Speed/Pause, busy/hold/Create/fatal, acknowledged-stop, generation, recovery and hidden-view guards remain authoritative.

**Screen-observable acceptance:** I can read the Author's premise and opening identity/family, then use Skip ahead directly while Playing or Paused to reach the next real stop with my chosen pace restored and no audit jargon.

## Requirement 2 — An Ongoing, Truthful Life Journal (LU-R2)

### User Story

As a player, I want ordinary Run to tell the dated story of my life, my circle, and the town, including what actually happened after decisions.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN Create, any Status (ordinary or stop), or an advancing reply succeeds, THE fact stream SHALL deliver opening-qualified bootstrap records or the new observed suffix of all 16 EventLog variants and native Player Chronicle outcomes through typed StoryBatch records. It SHALL retain actual actors including dead NPCs, exact values and causal EventIDs, world-bound event/Chronicle cursors, and distinct event/Chronicle identities; Worker delivery SHALL drain only after emit succeeds, not while constructing a snapshot.
2. DURING Run, THE default **Your life** stream SHALL select self, Close20, qualified actual past-life rememberers and town births/marriages/deaths using per-day start/end focus, while clearly labeling other actual news **Around town**. A rememberer SHALL qualify by kinship in either direction with the earlier body or absolute affinity ≥20 in the actual rememberer→earlier-body relation; weak rememberers SHALL remain available in people files/background news without redefining action eligibility. When primary news is quiet, background promotion SHALL be limited to approximately one real sentence per 5–6 wall seconds; contacts SHALL NOT be called friends merely because selected, and missing selected records SHALL NOT become a claim that nothing happened.
3. THE journal SHALL select the deck's eligible verbatim variants deterministically by actual EventId in fixed order, keeping replay words stable without RNG/model calls; Chronicle-only alternatives retain their actual append-index namespace. Social intensity bands SHALL choose the deck's low/medium/high wording without inventing changes. Public/Witnessed/Deathbed tag clauses SHALL be removed from narration while all raw tag facts remain retained. Actual player participants SHALL read you/your; kin phrases SHALL use event-time oriented relationships and recorded first names, not final-World guesses or parentheses. Older-life entries SHALL keep their original wording after rebirth; a later-life mention of an earlier body uses the deck's former-self phrase only when supported. Preserve inheritance estate-value, burned/credited offering, repayment and actual Dream-record meanings; unsupported complete lines are omitted/reported, never rewritten, qualified or linked to unrelated events.
4. WHEN a Player Chronicle outcome is delivered, THE journal SHALL explain the actual recorded choice, rules default or automatic resolution, chooser absence, and skipped parts/reasons, joining its native appended EventIDs without guessing by date/family. External source SHALL NOT be labeled model-generated, a recorded choice SHALL NOT imply every part applied, and linked choice/event facts SHALL remain separately stored while avoiding duplicate live narration.
5. THE **Your life** journal SHALL group entries under **Your life as {Full Name}** and **Between lives** for their actual life/Underworld phase, so sentences need no historical-life qualifiers and old entries do not change after rebirth. At bursts/fast paces it SHALL retain expandable factual day/week/Social/repayment groups, with milestones/choices explicit and true counts/date ranges/applicable sums. Read/Life entries remain complete sentences, not typewriter queues. All unmerged current-World facts/actors remain stored until successful new World, not reset at death/rebirth; only rendered groups are capped, initially around 120. Headings/ranges are presentation, not new core fields or fact eviction.
6. AFTER exact choice admission, THE main area SHALL show **Done. Press Play to see how it lands.** from deck section 5, not a preview as the result. WHEN that choice's actual Chronicle result arrives, THE section 4 result sentence SHALL replace the transition prominently in the main area, using actual node identity/appended links rather than guessing by day or upgrading an unjoined receipt. Its choice receipt SHALL then disappear; refusal keeps the current choice available.

**Screen-observable acceptance:** I can read dated you/your-family sentences under stable life headings, see my actual choice result replace its transition in the main area, and revisit an earlier life without rewritten wording or parenthetical qualifiers.

### Measured Planning Basis

The existing seed 1205, opening population 50, recorded-input first-life prefix contains 2,364 strict-before-own-death town events across 5,373 day bins; three bootstrap events, own death, and five subsequent same-day events are separate. Self + Close20 + town births/marriages/deaths deduplicates to 337 events: 6.27 per 100 days, 325 active and 5,048 zero selected-event days, maximum five in one day, rolling seven-/30-day maxima eight/12, and one/four selected events in the first 30/90 days. At Life pace this sample averages about one primary line per 3.2 seconds, not a guaranteed daily personal event.

Close20 includes kin or absolute affinity ≥20 in either direction; an all-existing-relations feed is not a close-friend feed. Two separate passive 1,000-day seed 1/2 samples yielded 409/515 town events and 44/52 related-close + vital events, not complete lifetimes. The measured 243,058-byte legacy lossy context-card output is not the future typed DTO's size or a hard transmission cap.

## Requirement 3 — Unmistakable Key Scenes and a Living Stage (LU-R3)

### User Story

As a player, I want key events to have large, readable performances and visible life transitions, while every game action remains immediately usable.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a key event occurs, THE stage SHALL retain the eight original families—family death, inheritance, debt due, feud, life stage, own death, rebirth and own Dream—and add the authorized **display-only kin-memorial variant of family death** in criterion 6, without creating a PlayerNode. Large actors/paperwork, meaningful movement, contrast, seconds-long pacing and gradual text SHALL make events apparent; actual Birth/Possession/Rebuild remain distinct, with an infant only for Birth. Other ordinary journal/town events SHALL NOT each become a cutscene.
2. THE stage SHALL expose immediate **Show all** and **Skip**, with full facts, a screen-reader-only full-text alternative and every legal option available from the start. The alternative SHALL NOT render a second visible scene-text copy. Skip reveals/settles presentation only, never Choose, pop a node, Run or Step; separate guarded **Next**/**Close** dismiss only an exact node-null entry, and valid actions cancel decoration without changing those actions.
3. WHEN lifecycle records arrive, THE performance order SHALL follow actual EventIDs, preserving historical dates/body/known ages and authoritative latest identity/actions. The permitted `last_dream` route uses its actual world-scoped resolution key/captured stop, never a fake EventID or input join. Ordinary ticks do not erase active historical performances; exact-node admission retires only its decision scene, rejection does not replay it, and current pending controls never wait behind history. For the kin memorial, ending/settling decorative work SHALL NOT end the card lifetime defined in criterion 6.
4. DURING ordinary Run, THE stage SHALL remain visibly alive through gentle reusable living/Underworld vignettes without invented weather, locations, or events and without changing RNG. Reduced motion SHALL immediately expose full text/final pictures; hidden views SHALL stop decorative work and, on return, adopt latest facts and settle elapsed work instead of draining a performance backlog.
5. THE stage SHALL retain its 448×192 logical/896×384 reference composition at 1280×720, but use the approved portrait-card direction rather than enlarging 16px full-body tokens as protagonists. Complete coordinated interiors/scenery with walls, windows and furniture SHALL replace flat color bands. At 1024-wide, narrow and 200%-zoom layouts, pictures compress before readable text/actions are clipped; content remains keyboard-scrollable. Composition caches depend on facts/context/age/assets, not full redraw each animation frame; avoid unsafe flashes/expensive blur. Art expansion follows the three-portrait/complete-certificate confirmation in Shared Boundaries.
6. WHEN an actual Death concerns the player's event-time **current-life spouse, parent, child or sibling**, and no actual family-death choice is offered for that Death, THE main stage SHALL automatically show the section 11 memorial without pausing, answering, spending or allocating input. After animation, Show all, Skip, reduced motion, hidden-view settlement or fast-forward truncation, the card SHALL remain until explicit **Close** or replacement by a genuine next scene; an ordinary tick, Status, vignette refresh or stop alone SHALL NOT clear it. WHEN the same recorded day includes actual Inheritance from that deceased to the player of that life, THE corresponding share-valued section 11 sentence SHALL join that same card without a second performance. Exact Death/Inheritance identities, deceased/heir/date and actual causes govern the join; late delivery updates only the same still-active card, without reopening a closed/replaced card. Corresponding journal entries and raw facts SHALL remain normally available. Real choices/lifecycle scenes and successful new World remain authoritative replacements, with all legal controls immediately usable.

**Screen-observable acceptance:** I see modern portrait cards and complete scenery supporting life changes; a close relative's memorial stays after Skip/fast-forward stops until I Close it or a genuine next scene replaces it, same-day inherited estate appears on that card, and both facts remain in the journal.

## Requirement 4 — People Files and Underworld Paperwork (LU-R4)

### User Story

As a player, I want identity, relationships, status, and afterlife actions to feel like a game's life notebook and fictional Underworld paperwork, not a pile of raw lists and forms.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE living notebook SHALL lead with identity, scene, choices, diary and portrait relationship cards. Choice scene/situation content is one presentation; Answer day/Resolution day are removed. Use section 11's single family-death scene and named buttons, section 5's Likely effects/default marker, and **replace only the player's Preview name with You**: retain numeric rows and add no directional phrase, qualifier or other text. Current ties/past-life rememberers remain distinct, no unsupported gauges or personal facts are invented, and the map stays secondary.
2. THE Underworld desk SHALL use copy-deck section 8's sections, folded or locally paged so the first screen shows the actual death certificate, joss balance and available actions. Dream/Haunt targets SHALL be compact keyboard-selectable portrait cards rather than repeated long lists; only an ineligible person's card shows its reason. All legal targets remain reachable. Death/name/date/known-age and actual cost/ruling facts remain authoritative; no death cause is invented, and death → Underworld → actual new-body transitions remain distinct.
3. THE game interface SHALL preserve all 24 player options, Bribe, Haunt, all six Dream intents and eight outcomes, all three rebirth paths and every available birth slot, personality carry, all seven traits, and paid Set/Adjust customization in a complete personal-file workspace. Prices, Needed conditions, eligibility, and unavailable reasons SHALL remain understandable and complete rather than be hidden for styling.
4. THE paperwork SHALL use the deck's status words only, preserving actual sending/considered/received/processed/refused/cancelled/unknown distinctions and exact correlated result carriers separately from current World facts. Without an input-sequence/request-correlated result, a receipt SHALL NOT upgrade from Received to Processed, but MAY expire from the player view: choice receipts disappear when their corresponding Chronicle result appears; other receipts show only for the actual current life or current Underworld phase and expire at death/rebirth. Hiding a receipt SHALL NOT delete facts, replay or technical diagnostics, imply completion, spend money or undo a pending operation. Same-generation reads do not blanket-disable operations; final admission remains the core's decision.
5. THE copy and compositions SHALL use the Author deck verbatim without extra glosses, repeated disclaimers or independent punchlines. Humor targets fictional bureaucracy rather than ancestor practitioners; joss paper/spirit money remain respectful. Secondary map/technical disclosures do not displace current actions, and callbacks/input semantics remain unchanged by card, stamp or portrait styling.

**Screen-observable acceptance:** The Underworld's first screen shows my certificate, balance and actions; I can select Dream/Haunt people compactly, reach every legal operation, and see only current-phase paperwork rather than receipts accumulated across lives.
